# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place for traders and developers at every level.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give specific and constructive feedback, credit other people's work, and assume good intent.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks; discrimination of any kind; spam or unsolicited promotion; sharing someone's private information; pressuring anyone into trades, purchases or live trading.

**Enforcement:** moderators may edit, hide, lock or delete posts and may block accounts that break this code or the [community rules](COMMUNITY_RULES.md). Report problems with GitHub's **Report content** option or by email to info@b4signals.com. Reports are handled privately.
