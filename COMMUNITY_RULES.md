# Community rules

These rules keep the B4 Indicators community useful, legal and safe. Moderators may edit, lock or remove posts that break them.

## 1. Honest results only
- No profit claims, win-rate boasts, screenshots of account balances, "guaranteed" or "can't lose" language.
- When you share results, say how you got them: instrument, bar type, dates, simulation or Playback, costs included or not.
- Past and simulated results do not predict future results. Say so when you post them.

## 2. Strategies are shared for simulation
- Strategies posted here must be set to **Simulation and Playback accounts only** (the B4 Studio default for public strategies), with a protective stop and daily loss limit.
- Do not tell anyone to run a community strategy on a live account. Nobody here gives personalized trading or investment advice.

## 3. Code you have the right to share
- Post only code you wrote or are allowed to share, under **MIT, Apache-2.0 or MPL-2.0**.
- Keep the original author's name and license when you build on someone's work, and say what you changed.
- No decompiled, leaked, cracked or paid third-party code, and no license keys.

## 4. No proprietary B4 code
- B4's paid tools and their internal logic (B4 Algo, confirmations, CoPilot Pro, Indicator Pro and similar) are not posted, requested or reverse-engineered here.
- Building on the **public B4 Signal API** and the free developer kit is welcome.

## 5. Safe code
- No code that reaches the internet, starts programs, writes or deletes files, or reads account credentials. B4 Studio's static checks flag these; please don't work around them.
- Never post passwords, API keys, account numbers or personal data.

## 6. Be decent
- Critique the code, not the person. Help beginners. No spam, self-promotion links or selling.
- See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## 7. Not a B4 release
Community builds are not reviewed for correctness, profitability or live-trading safety, and are not endorsed by B4 or TopTech Digital LLC. Test everything yourself.

Report a problem: use the **Report content** option on any post, or email info@b4signals.com.
